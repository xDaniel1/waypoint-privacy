Waypoint Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Waypoint is a maps and navigation app developed by Daniel Lantigua ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Information the app uses

  • Precise location. Used to center the map, show nearby places, give turn-by-turn directions and, if you allow "Always" access, keep a trip going while another app is open. Your location is sent to our service providers (below) only to return places, routes, weather and speed limits.
  • Search queries. Text and voice searches are sent to Google Places to return results. Voice audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition and is not stored or sent to us.
  • Account details (only if you sign in). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name, email address and an account ID so we can keep your account.
  • Saved content (only if you sign in). Favorites, saved places and recent searches are stored in your account so they sync across your devices.

Service providers

To work, the app sends the minimum necessary data to: Mapbox (map tiles and routing; the Mapbox SDK also collects anonymous usage telemetry, which you can opt out of using the ⓘ button on the map), Google (Places search, Sign in with Google), Apple (WeatherKit, Sign in with Apple, maps data), Supabase (account and sync storage), Open-Meteo (weather) and OpenStreetMap Overpass (speed limits). Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or the advertising identifier.
  • We do not upload crash reports; diagnostic reports stay on your device.

Retention and deletion

Data you sync to your account is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete your account. To request deletion of your account and its data, email the address below.

Children

Waypoint is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
